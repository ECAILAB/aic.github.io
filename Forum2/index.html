    2026 International Pre-Conference Workshop on Artificial Intelligence and Computing (Pre-AIC 2026)

    The 1st International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Computing (AIC 2026) – South Korea Workshop is a pre-conference event affiliated with the inaugural AIC 2026. It will be held in Ansan City, South Korea, from April 7–12, 2026, immediately preceding the main conference.

    Pre-AIC 2026 provides a high-level international forum for innovative academics and industrial experts to present recent advances in artificial-intelligence theory and applications. The workshop serves as a vital platform for scholarly exchange and academic collaboration, setting the stage for the subsequent main conference discussions and giving researchers opportunities to share preliminary findings and forge new partnerships.

    Organization

    • Program Committee:
      • Zhihui Zhan, Nankai University, China

    Key Topics

    The program will feature keynote speeches and sessions devoted to key topics such as:

    • Evolutionary-computation theory and applications
    • Multi-objective optimization for scheduling problems
    • Hybrid intelligent algorithms
    • Machine-learning-based scheduling systems
    • Real-world implementations of AI-driven scheduling solutions across diverse industrial sectors
